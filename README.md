# Fintex-Solution
**Fintex Solutions** is a business-oriented website created using WordPress and the Divi theme to showcase a modern B2B trading and supply company based in KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. The site is designed to communicate the company’s services, products, and value proposition to potential clients and partners in an engaging, accessible way.
Project Overview

Fintex Solutions is positioned as a dynamic trading and procurement business that connects buyers with trusted suppliers. The website highlights core services such as product sourcing, supply & distribution, bulk procurement, and customized trade solutions — making B2B trade faster, easier, and more reliable. 

## 🧠 Key Features

- 🧩 **Responsive Layout** — Adaptive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- 📦 **Service & Product Showcase** — Clear presentation of the company’s capabilities and offerings.
- 📄 **About & Mission Sections** — Communicates company vision, values, and story.
- 📬 **Contact & Quotation Form** — Allows users to easily get in touch for inquiries and quotes. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## 🛠️ Built With

- **WordPress** — Content management system.  
- **Divi Theme** — Page builder and design framework.  
- **Custom Content & Layouts** — Created visually using Divi’s visual builder.
